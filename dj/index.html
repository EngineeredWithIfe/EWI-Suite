New to DJing? Here's the whole idea in four lines.

  • Load two tracks — one to each deck (a file, or paste a public link).
  • Match the tempo — press SYNC on the deck you're bringing in; Decks matches its BPM to the other deck and lines up the beats.
  • Blend — start the incoming deck, then slide the crossfader across while easing the Low EQ to swap basslines cleanly.
  • Play — drop hot cues, roll a beat loop, sweep the filter, or add echo/reverb. Toggle Instrumental to duck the vocal for an a-cappella blend.
