EWI Cut — guide

A compact CapCut-style editor. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Build

Import photos, video or audio (or generate a Title, Color card, or Tone). Click a pool item to drop it on the timeline at the playhead.

Edit on the timeline

Drag a clip to move it; drag its edges to trim. Move the playhead by clicking the ruler. Split cuts the selected clip at the playhead. Layers stack bottom→top (video base, overlays, titles).

Style

The Inspector controls position, scale, rotation, opacity, fade in/out, speed, volume, brightness/contrast/saturation, and Ken Burns zoom for photos. Titles have font, colour, and an animation.

Export

Export video plays the timeline once and records a high-quality MP4 (H.264 video + mixed AAC audio). Save frame captures the current preview as a PNG.

Keyboard

Space play/pause · S split · D duplicate · X/Delete delete · ←/→ step.
