Flow Dictation runs on your Mac

Flow Dictation does real work on your own machine — processing that a static web host can’t (and shouldn’t) do for you. Your data never leaves your device. Start it in one command, then open it locally:

cd working-samples/02-flow-dictation
python3 server.py
# then open http://localhost:8702
